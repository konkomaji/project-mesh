Legal & policies
Privacy

Betar never collects a phone number or an email address. There is no server, so there is no server-side log of who you talk to or when.

Your identity is a key pair stored on this device. Panic wipe clears it instantly, with nothing sent anywhere first.

Terms

Betar is open source under AGPL-3.0. Anyone can read the code, run their own relay, or build on it. No account to agree to, no fine print to sign.

Content policy

No server or relay can delete a post once the mesh has carried it. What your own app shows you is still up to you: the Community Notes layer lets every person locally hide or warn on content their own trusted network has flagged.

Illegal content gets no special server-side removal, because no server exists to remove it from. This is a stated limit of the architecture, not an oversight.

Reading this mockup

  • Tap the bottom tabs to switch Feed, Nearby, Chats, Notes and You.
  • Feed opens on a room scoped to your block (#blk-9q8yy), with city and region rooms one tap away.
  • The composer's image button attaches a mesh-local preview only, full resolution arrives once a relay bridge is reached.
  • The chat thread's pin button shares a location once, never a live, ongoing position.
  • Tap 1 note on a feed post, or the Notes tab, to see the Community Notes layout.
  • Red is reserved for Panic wipe only, everywhere else in this concept.
