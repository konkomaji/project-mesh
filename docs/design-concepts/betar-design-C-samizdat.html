Betar: Design Concept C of 4

Samizdat

Underground press, not startup gloss No. 03, censorship resistant by construction

A design system built to look and feel like an independent press, not a social app: typewriter rules, newsprint paper, one color that only ever means alert, redaction, or emergency. The point is credibility to journalists and activists first, delight second. If it looked like every other feed, it would not earn the trust it is asking for.

01 / Color

Palette

Mostly black ink on newsprint paper. Exactly one accent, a press red used only for alerts, destructive actions, and the Community Note stamp, so it never gets diluted into decoration. No tricolor flag combinations anywhere in this system, on purpose.

02 / Type

Type Scale

Condensed bold uppercase for headlines in the masthead voice, a plain grotesque for body copy, monospace for timestamps, IDs, and anything that is effectively data. Plain UI labels (tabs, toggles, placeholders) stay sentence case, never tracked uppercase. No decorative or script typefaces anywhere.

05 / Structure

Mobile App Mockup

Same information architecture across all four concepts, for fair comparison: Feed, Nearby, Chats, Profile as the four persistent tabs, with Chat Thread, Post Detail, Opening, and Legal & Policies as drill-down screens that drop the tab bar for a back arrow or a single continue action, same as any native app. Feed content is scoped to geohash rooms (block, city, or region precision), not one undifferentiated stream.

← Legal & Policies
Privacy
Betar collects nothing. There is no server to log what you do. No phone number or email is tied to your identity. No analytics are sent anywhere, by this app or by us.
Terms
Betar is free and open source, licensed AGPL-3.0. Read the code. Change it. Run your own build. There is no company behind this to sue, subpoena, or pressure into a backdoor.
Content Policy
No one can delete a post once it reaches the mesh or a relay. There is no server that could do it, even under a legal order. What exists instead: Community Notes let any reader flag a post, and your own app can hide or warn on flagged content using your own trust network, computed locally, on your device only.
