Legal and policies

Privacy

Betar never asks for a phone number or an email address. There is no server, so there are no server logs of who you talked to or what you posted.

Location is only ever shared when you tap the share button in a chat, once, for that message alone.

Terms

Betar is free and open source, licensed under AGPL-3.0. Anyone can read the code, change it, or run their own version.

There is no company behind this app and no account to agree to a contract with. Using Betar means agreeing to carry and relay other people's encrypted messages as part of the mesh.

Content policy

No one, including the people who built Betar, can delete a post or message once it has spread across the mesh or the relay network. There is no server to remove it from.

Each person's own app can locally hide or warn on content flagged through Community Notes. This runs on your device only and never removes anything for anyone else.

How to read this mockup

  • Tap the bottom tab bar to switch between Feed, Nearby, Chats and Profile.
  • Tap a post with a Community note badge, or any chat row, to open its detail screen.
  • Feed posts carry a geohash room tag (for example #oldtown, city precision) instead of one undifferentiated global feed.
  • The round button on Feed starts a new post, including an optional image. A mesh-only image stays a small preview until a relay bridge is reached.
  • The pin button in a chat thread sends a one time location, not a continuous live share.
  • Nearby shows local mesh peers as a small network graph plus relay bridge status in plain language.
  • Profile carries the panic wipe control, kept visually distinct and separate from ordinary settings.
