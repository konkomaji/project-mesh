Design concept A

Ground Signal

Betar should look like infrastructure you can trust precisely because it hides nothing. Every screen shows what is encrypted, how a message is travelling, and how many hops stand between you and the person you are talking to. Near-black surfaces and a single signal-green accent keep the interface calm in low light and readable in the field, closer to field radio and terminal tooling than to mainstream social apps.

Legal & policies

Privacy

Betar never collects a phone number or email, because there is no account to attach one to. There is no server, so there is no server-side log of who you talked to or what you posted. Your peer ID is a public key generated on this device.

Terms

Betar is free, open source software under AGPL-3.0. No company runs it and no company can shut it down. You run the mesh by running the app. Use of relay bridges you do not control is at your own judgment, same as any public network.

Content policy

No server and no relay operator can delete a post once it has spread through the mesh. There is no central takedown.

What exists instead: Community Notes, rated by many peers with no single reviewer, and a local filter on your own device. Flagged content can be hidden or warned on in your own app, by your own choice, without anyone deleting it for everyone.

This is a real limit, not a loophole. Read it plainly before you rely on this app for anything sensitive.
